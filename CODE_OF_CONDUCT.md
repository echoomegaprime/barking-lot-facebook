# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to interact professionally and respectfully.
Harassment, discriminatory language, and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull
requests, commits, or any other project space.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and when representing the
project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **security@echo-op.com**. Maintainers
will review and respond, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this
standard, or temporarily or permanently restrict a contributor's access.
